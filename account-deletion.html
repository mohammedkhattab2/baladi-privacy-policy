# Baladi Account Deletion Request ## Delete Your Baladi Account If you want to delete your Baladi account and associated personal data, you can request account deletion using the instructions below. ## How to Request Account Deletion To request deletion of your account: 1. Open the Baladi application. 2. Go to: **Settings → Account → Delete Account** 3. Confirm the deletion request. If you cannot access the application, you can contact us using the support email provided on our Google Play Store listing and request account deletion. Please include: * Your registered phone number. * Your account name. * A request to delete your Baladi account. ## Data That Will Be Deleted When your account is deleted, we will delete or anonymize personal data associated with your account, including: * Name. * Phone number. * Saved addresses. * Profile information. * Authentication information. * User preferences. * Push notification identifiers. ## Data That May Be Retained Some information may be retained when required for legitimate business or legal purposes, including: * Order records required for accounting and transaction history. * Fraud prevention and security records. Retained data is stored only for the period required by applicable regulations and then deleted or anonymized. ## Account Deletion Processing Account deletion requests are processed after verification of account ownership. Once deletion is completed, you will no longer be able to access your account. ## Contact For questions about account deletion, please contact the Baladi support team through the contact information available on the Google Play Store listing.
