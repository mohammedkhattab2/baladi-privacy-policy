Baladi Privacy Policy

Effective Date: June 30, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Baladi, a daily delivery platform connecting customers with local shops and delivery riders.

We are committed to protecting your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, when we share it, and the choices available to you.

2. Who We Are

Baladi is the data controller responsible for processing your personal data in order to provide ordering, delivery, notifications, loyalty points, and referral services.

3. Scope

This policy applies to all Baladi mobile applications and related services including messaging, location services, and delivery operations.

4. Information We Collect

Account Information

  • Full Name
  • Phone Number
  • Encrypted PIN

Location Information

  • Saved delivery addresses

We never collect background location.

Order Information

  • Products
  • Prices
  • Order history
  • Ratings

Messages & Attachments

  • Chat messages
  • Uploaded images
  • Purchase request attachments

Loyalty Information

  • Points balance
  • Referral code
  • Referral rewards

Technical Information

  • Firebase Cloud Messaging Token
  • Device model
  • Operating system
  • App version
  • Diagnostic logs

  • Create and manage your account

  • Send push notifications
