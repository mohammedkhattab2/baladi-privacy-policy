Privacy Policy Effective date: June 30, 2026 Last updated: June 30, 2026 1. Introduction Welcome to Baladi, a daily delivery platform connecting you with local shops and riders. We are committed to protecting your privacy. This policy explains what data we collect, how we use it, with whom we share it, and your choices. By using the app, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy. If you do not agree, please stop using the service. 2. Who we are Baladi is the data controller responsible for processing your personal data for ordering, delivery, push notifications, loyalty points, and referral programs. For privacy inquiries, use in‑app support or the contact details in the “Contact” section below. 3. Scope This policy applies to the Baladi mobile apps and related services, including in‑app chat and address selection on the map. 4. Data we collect We collect the minimum data necessary to operate and improve the service: Account data: full name, phone number, encrypted PIN, and encrypted security question/answer for account recovery. Delivery and location data: Saved delivery addresses and delivery instructions. Precise location (via device GPS) only when you grant permission and while using location features (e.g., picking delivery location on the map, rider readiness checks). We do not collect background location. Order data: items, quantities, prices, order status/history, and service ratings (if provided). Communications and attachments: messages you send and images you choose to attach (camera or gallery), e.g., purchase requests. Loyalty and referral data: points balance, transactions, referral code, and referral status. Device and technical data: push notification token (FCM), device type/OS, app version, and diagnostic logs for reliability and troubleshooting. Usage data: non‑sensitive interaction events (e.g., screens viewed, searches) to improve user experience and app quality; no third‑party ads SDKs. We do not collect payment card data because payments are cash‑on‑delivery unless stated otherwise. 5. How we use your data We use data to: Create/manage your account and verify identity at login/recovery. Process orders, transmit them to shops, and coordinate delivery with riders. Send push notifications about orders and important updates (when enabled). Operate loyalty and referral programs. Prevent fraud/abuse and protect security. Perform internal analytics on app performance, content relevance, and operations (no third‑party ads profiling). Comply with legal obligations. 6. Legal bases (where applicable) Contract performance: to provide ordering and delivery services. Consent: for optional features such as location access, notifications, camera/gallery attachments. Legitimate interests: service improvement, security, and fraud prevention, without overriding your rights. Legal obligation: when required by law or valid authority request. 7. Data sharing We do not sell your personal data. We may share data with: Shops: to fulfill your order (name, phone, order details, address as needed). Delivery riders: to complete delivery (name, phone, address, delivery notes). Service providers (“processors”) under confidentiality and security obligations, including: Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google) for push notifications. Google Maps SDK for location features and map rendering. Cloud media storage (e.g., Cloudinary) for images you choose to upload. Hosting, logging, and email/SMS providers as needed. Authorities: when required by applicable law or court order. We do not share data with third‑party ad networks and do not use Advertising ID for ads. 8. Third‑party services Some features rely on third‑party SDKs/APIs: Firebase Cloud Messaging (Google): delivers push notifications; stores an FCM token linked to your device. Google Maps SDK: renders maps and may receive your IP and map interactions to deliver tiles/locations. Cloud media storage (e.g., Cloudinary): securely stores images you choose to upload. These providers process data on our behalf and are bound by appropriate data protection terms. 9. Permissions and your choices Location (While in Use): to pick delivery location on the map and for rider readiness/position updates; you can deny it and set the address manually. Camera and Photos/Media: to attach images (e.g., purchase requests or shop product images); optional. Notifications: to receive order updates and important alerts; you can disable them in system settings. Local notifications: used to display alerts while the app is foregrounded; configurable in system settings. You can manage permissions at any time in your device settings. 10. Storage and security We implement reasonable technical and organizational measures, including encryption for PINs and security answers, access controls, and periodic security reviews. Sensitive tokens are stored using platform‑secure storage where available. No internet transmission is 100% secure—do not share your PIN. 11. Data retention and deletion We retain your data while your account is active or as needed to provide the service. Certain records may be retained longer where required by law, for dispute resolution, or fraud prevention. When you delete your account via the in‑app feature, we work to delete or anonymize personal data per our policies and applicable law, while retaining what is legally required. 12. Children’s privacy The service is for users 18+ or minors with parental consent. We do not knowingly collect data from children without appropriate consent; we will delete such data if discovered. 13. Your rights Subject to applicable law, you may: Access your personal data and request a copy. Correct inaccurate data via account settings or support. Request deletion via the in‑app delete account feature. Object to certain processing or withdraw consent without affecting prior lawful processing. 14. Cookies and tracking The mobile app does not use browser cookies. Technical identifiers (e.g., FCM token) may be used to deliver notifications and enhance performance/security. 15. Data Safety (Google Play mapping) Data collected: precise location (only when using location features), personal info (name, phone), app activity/usage (non‑sensitive interactions), device or other IDs (FCM token), photos/media you choose to upload, and order info. Purposes: core app functionality (ordering/delivery, notifications), account management, fraud prevention, security, and internal analytics. Data sharing: with shops/riders to fulfill orders and with service providers under contract; no sale of data; no third‑party ads. Optional features: location, notifications, and camera/photos can be disabled in system settings. 16. Changes to this policy We may update this policy; material changes will be communicated in‑app or via appropriate means. The “Last updated” date reflects the latest version. 17. Contact In‑app support within Baladi. Email or phone listed in “About the app” or support settings. We aim to respond within a reasonable timeframe consistent with legal requirements.
